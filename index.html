About Everest Metal Fabrications

At Everest Metal Fabrications, we are dedicated to fabricating and installing high-quality metal solutions. We are known for our transparent communication, punctuality, and commitment to every project.

We work closely with contractors, builders, and owners, offering durable products, efficient processes, and reliable service. Our approach is simple: do things right, from the first contact to the final delivery.

Frequently Asked Questions

We answer your main questions before starting your project.

Do you work nationwide or just regionally?

We have the logistics and infrastructure to develop and install projects nationwide. Please contact us to review viability at your specific location.

Do you offer warranties on roofing projects?

Yes, we guarantee our work. The warranty period varies depending on the materials and scope of the roofing system (usually between 10 and 20 years for full waterproofing).

Is your crew insured and safety-trained?

Yes. We are registered with WSIB Ontario and are members of the Toronto Construction Association. Our crew works with certified fall-protection equipment and follows strict safety procedures on every site.
